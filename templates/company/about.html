About MailCurrent.io

Empowering developers with reliable, scalable email delivery solutions.

Our Mission

At MailCurrent.io, we believe that email communication should be simple, reliable, and powerful. Our mission is to provide developers and businesses with the tools they need to deliver exceptional email experiences to their users.

We're committed to making email delivery as straightforward as sending a simple API request, while providing enterprise-grade reliability and comprehensive analytics to help you understand and optimize your email performance.

What We Do

Email API

RESTful API for sending transactional emails with templates, tracking, and analytics.

Analytics & Tracking

Comprehensive email analytics including opens, clicks, and delivery status.

Template Management

Create, manage, and version control email templates with variable substitution.

Webhooks & Integration

Real-time notifications and seamless integration with your existing systems.
